# Hideout data review — 25 October 2026

The tracker still covers **15 stations, 36 listed upgrades, and 72 physical materials plus cash**. Every listed upgrade was checked again: materials, quantities, cash costs, previous levels, capability requirements, and benefits. The current database matches the existing catalogue; no confirmed recipe-cost changes were found.

The [GZW Hideout database](https://gzwhideout.com/) remains the selected recipe source. The [community spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRQRTtotA1PTfVGgtAfh3eJDQLsJaS4YrXRma_ytUNETwNFoCeu-rQslY5JJlTCpJ9xuqaA8v6iujqM/pubhtml?gid=0&single=true) has expanded since the first review and now lists all 15 stations. Comparing its individual recipes revealed two additional differences. Neither source has been independently verified in-game by this tracker.

## Five differences still need confirmation

| Upgrade | Tracker / database | Community spreadsheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Filtration level 2](https://gzwhideout.com/laboratory/filtration/2) | 3 Fine Metal Mesh | 2 Fine Metal Mesh |
| [Micro Electronics level 2](https://gzwhideout.com/workshop/micro-electronics/2) | 2 Soldering Iron | 1 Soldering Iron |
| [Advanced Ventilation](https://gzwhideout.com/laboratory/ventilation-system/3) | Level 3, directly after level 1 | Listed in the level 2 column |
| [Laboratory Storage level 2](https://gzwhideout.com/laboratory/storage-equipment/2) | 1 Electrical Parts | Not listed |
| [Workshop Hardware level 3](https://gzwhideout.com/workshop/hardware/3) | 5 Electrical Parts | 1 Electrical Parts |

The last two warnings are new. The missing spreadsheet entry is an omission, not confirmation of a zero cost. Existing quantities remain selected while the warnings make these disagreements visible. Ventilation keeps the database's explicit level 1 → level 3 progression.

For all listed station upgrades combined, selected totals remain **$340,000**, **92 Steel**, **38 Electrical Parts**, and **8 Circuit Board**, across **46 physical upgrade materials**. The database's general item-tracker totals also include crafting and production needs; those extra quantities are not added to station build costs.

## Official game information

The [official Rogue Ops release changelog](https://www.grayzonewarfare.com/news/rogue-ops-out-now-changelog) identifies the release as **0.5.0.1** and describes rooms, stations, power, crafting, and how resources are obtained. It does not list individual upgrade quantities.

The **23 October backend hotfix**, reviewed in [MADFINGER's Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2479810/allnews/?l=english), addresses rank-earned stash space and queue connection failures. It lists no hideout recipe changes. This does not rule out undocumented changes.

The [original community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/GrayZoneWarfare/comments/1xcb4az/list_of_materials_for_the_hideout_lets/) includes additional player reports and later-tier entries. Those reports do not resolve the five differences above.

## What updates for players

The catalogue date, references, and warnings update automatically in connected copies of the tracker. Saved quantities, completed levels, goals, and cloud saves are unchanged. Version 1.3.3 also makes the Data & sources heading follow the current warning count and shows a short research summary.

If an in-game requirement differs, use **Feedback → Report a bug** and include the station, level, game version, and a screenshot of its requirement panel. Avoid attaching private save backups.
