# Hideout data review — 25 October 2026

The tracker covers **15 stations, 36 listed upgrades, and 72 physical materials plus cash**. Every listed upgrade was checked again: materials, quantities, cash costs, previous levels, capability requirements, and benefits. The current database matches the existing catalogue; no confirmed recipe-cost changes were found.

The [GZW Hideout database](https://gzwhideout.com/) remains the selected recipe source. The [community spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRQRTtotA1PTfVGgtAfh3eJDQLsJaS4YrXRma_ytUNETwNFoCeu-rQslY5JJlTCpJ9xuqaA8v6iujqM/pubhtml?gid=0&single=true) and the updated [Fandom hideout wiki](https://gray-zone-warfare.fandom.com/wiki/Hideout) were also compared across all 15 stations and 36 upgrade rows. The three-source review records **17 flagged differences** in costs, numbering and benefits. No source was independently verified in-game by this tracker.

Storage benefit warnings do not change shopping-list totals.

The wiki's [page history](https://gray-zone-warfare.fandom.com/wiki/Hideout?action=history) shows an edit at **00:04 on 25 October 2026**, described as **“typo.”** It lists the station-table addition on **24 October**, followed by disassembly-recipe additions. Specific revision diffs require a Fandom login and were not inspected; the comparison above uses the currently visible page. The site's general banner says the wiki is no longer updated, although this page's history clearly records recent edits. The page itself still contains placeholders and a repeated level label.

The wiki's disassembly recipes are a separate activity and are not added to station construction costs or advertised as a supported tracker feature.

For all listed station upgrades combined, selected totals remain **$340,000**, **92 Steel**, **38 Electrical Parts**, and **8 Circuit Board**, across **46 physical upgrade materials**. The database's general item-tracker totals also include crafting and production needs; those extra quantities are not added to station build costs.

## Official game information

The [official Rogue Ops release changelog](https://www.grayzonewarfare.com/news/rogue-ops-out-now-changelog) identifies the release as **0.5.0.1** and describes rooms, stations, power, crafting, and how resources are obtained. It does not list individual upgrade quantities.

The **23 October backend hotfix**, reviewed in [MADFINGER's Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2479810/allnews/?l=english), addresses rank-earned stash space and queue connection failures. It lists no hideout recipe changes. This does not rule out undocumented changes.

The [original community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/GrayZoneWarfare/comments/1xcb4az/list_of_materials_for_the_hideout_lets/) includes additional player reports and later-tier entries. Those reports do not resolve every source difference above.

## What updates for players

The references and warnings update automatically in connected copies of the tracker. Saved quantities, completed levels, goals, and cloud saves are unchanged. Version 1.3.4 labels database, spreadsheet and wiki links correctly and includes room names to distinguish the two Storage Equipment stations. Existing apps can receive the catalogue corrections without downloading a new EXE; the latest EXE also includes the clearer source labels.

If an in-game requirement differs, use **Feedback → Report a bug** and include the station, level, game version, and a screenshot of its requirement panel. Avoid attaching private save backups.
